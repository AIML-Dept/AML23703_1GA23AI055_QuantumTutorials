State |+> measured in X-basis over 2000 shots -> counts: {'0': 2000}
State |-> measured in X-basis over 2000 shots -> counts: {'1': 2000}

Interpretation:
- |+> measured in the X-basis should collapse (near) deterministically to outcome '0'.
- |-> measured in the X-basis should collapse (near) deterministically to outcome '1'.
- Any small deviation from 100%/0% is purely simulator shot noise, since these are exact eigenstates of the X operator.
