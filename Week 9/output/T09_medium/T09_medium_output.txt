Key observations:
  * Phi+ and Phi- both give only 00/11 -> identical in the Z basis.
  * Psi+ and Psi- both give only 01/10 -> identical in the Z basis.
  * The +/- relative phase is invisible to a Z-basis measurement;
    it only shows up in a different basis (see Exercise 3).
  * Psi- prints as -|01> + |10>: this is (|01> - |10>)/sqrt(2) times a
    global phase of -1, which has no physical effect.
