 SUMMARY
======================================================================
Scenario                            QBER    CHSH S   Result
No eavesdropper                    0.00%     2.840   key accepted
With Eve (intercept-resend)       24.59%     1.403   key rejected

How entanglement secures the channel:
  * Phi+ gives identical results when Alice and Bob use the same basis,
    in Z AND in X, so the matched rounds form a shared random key.
  * The key does not exist until it is measured, so there is nothing
    for Eve to copy in transit, and no-cloning stops her duplicating the qubit.
  * If Eve measures, she breaks the entanglement: errors appear in the
    basis she guessed wrong (~25% QBER) and S drops to the classical range.
  * Alice and Bob detect this publicly and discard the key.
