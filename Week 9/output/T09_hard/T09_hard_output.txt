[A] Phi+ measured in the Z basis vs the X (Hadamard) basis

Z-basis circuit:
        ┌───┐      ░ ┌─┐   
   q_0: ┤ H ├──■───░─┤M├───
        └───┘┌─┴─┐ ░ └╥┘┌─┐
   q_1: ─────┤ X ├─░──╫─┤M├
             └───┘ ░  ║ └╥┘
meas: 2/══════════════╩══╩═
                      0  1 

X-basis circuit (H on both qubits before measurement):
        ┌───┐     ┌───┐ ░ ┌─┐   
   q_0: ┤ H ├──■──┤ H ├─░─┤M├───
        └───┘┌─┴─┐├───┤ ░ └╥┘┌─┐
   q_1: ─────┤ X ├┤ H ├─░──╫─┤M├
             └───┘└───┘ ░  ║ └╥┘
meas: 2/═══════════════════╩══╩═
                           0  1 

Z basis counts : {'00': 515, '11': 509}   E_ZZ = +1.000
X basis counts : {'00': 515, '11': 509}   E_XX = +1.000
-> Phi+ = (|++> + |-->)/sqrt(2): the qubits agree in BOTH bases.

[B] Correlation of all four Bell states in different bases

State         E_ZZ      E_XX      E_ZX   X-basis counts
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Phi+        +1.000    +1.000    -0.053   {'00': 515, '11': 509}
Phi-        +1.000    -1.000    -0.008   {'01': 526, '10': 498}
Psi+        -1.000    +1.000    -0.053   {'00': 526, '11': 498}
Psi-        -1.000    -1.000    -0.008   {'01': 526, '10': 498}

Reading the table:
  * Phi+ : agree in Z (+1), agree in X (+1)
  * Phi- : agree in Z (+1), DISAGREE in X (-1)  <- the minus sign shows up
  * Psi+ : disagree in Z (-1), agree in X (+1)
  * Psi- : disagree in Z (-1), disagree in X (-1)  (anti-correlated in every basis)
  * Mixed bases (Z on one, X on the other): E ~ 0, no correlation at all.
  * Phi+/Phi- looked identical in Exercise 2 (Z basis); the X basis
    tells them apart. Same for Psi+/Psi-.

[C] Classical look-alike: a source that sends |00> or |11> at random
    (50/50 classical mixture - no entanglement, no superposition)
    ZZ basis counts : {'00': 512, '11': 512}   E = +1.000
    XX basis counts : {'00': 246, '01': 242, '10': 267, '11': 269}   E = +0.006
-> The classical mixture copies Phi+ perfectly in the Z basis (E=+1),
   but in the X basis the correlation vanishes (E~0).
   Phi+ stays perfectly correlated (E=+1) in X too. Correlation that
   survives a change of basis is the fingerprint of entanglement.
